The Diagnostic Triad in Sleep Medicine

Class 0 • Healthy

Optimal Restorative Sleep

Sleep duration between 7.0–8.5 hours with high subjective quality, normative blood pressure (<120/80 mmHg), balanced autonomic parasympathetic tone, and low stress.

Class 1 • Insomnia

Chronic Sleep Onset & Maintenance

Characterized by severe autonomic hyperarousal (Stress ≥ 7), shortened sleep duration (<6h), elevated resting heart rate (>75 bpm), and non-restorative delta sleep fragmentation.

Class 2 • Sleep Apnea

Obstructive Pharyngeal Collapse

Directly correlated with elevated BMI (Overweight / Obese), Stage 1 or 2 Hypertension (Systolic ≥ 130 mmHg), and daytime exhaustion resulting from nocturnal oxygen desaturations.
